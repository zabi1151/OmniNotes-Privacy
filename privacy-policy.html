Privacy Policy for OmniNotes

Effective date: October 11, 2026

OmniNotes is built by Zabi.Dev. This Privacy Policy explains how OmniNotes collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

OmniNotes may process the following information depending on the features you use:

  • Account information: Google sign-in information such as your email address and account identifier, when you choose to sign in.
  • Notes and content: note titles, note text, tags, reminders, drawings, images, and voice attachments that you create in the app.
  • Local security settings: app lock, vault lock, and biometric unlock preferences stored on your device.
  • Purchase information: subscription or lifetime purchase status verified through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.
  • Advertising data: ad identifiers, consent status, and ad interaction signals processed by Google AdMob where applicable.
  • Device and diagnostic data: basic technical information needed for authentication, sync, purchases, ads, and app functionality.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide note creation, editing, reminders, attachments, vault, and local storage features.
  • Authenticate users with Google sign-in.
  • Sync premium user notes and attachments across devices through Supabase.
  • Verify subscriptions, lifetime purchases, and premium access through RevenueCat and Google Play Billing.
  • Show ads to free users through Google AdMob, where permitted by your consent and applicable law.
  • Prevent abuse, protect user data, and maintain app functionality.

3. Local Storage and Cloud Sync

OmniNotes is designed as an offline-first notes app. Notes are stored locally on your device. Local note payloads and attachments are encrypted before being saved in the local database.

Premium users may enable cloud sync. When cloud sync is active, notes and attachments are stored in Supabase under your authenticated account. Cloud data is protected by Supabase authentication, row-level security, private storage rules, and TLS. Cloud notes are not end-to-end encrypted from the server.

4. Third-Party Services

OmniNotes uses third-party services to provide core app features:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, storage, realtime sync, and backend functions.
  • Google Sign-In / Google Play Services: account sign-in and Android platform services.
  • Google Play Billing: subscription and lifetime purchase processing.
  • RevenueCat: purchase verification, subscription status, and entitlement management.
  • Google AdMob and Google UMP: advertising, consent collection, ad delivery, and ad measurement for free users.

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

Free users may see banner and interstitial ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where required, OmniNotes requests consent using Google’s User Messaging Platform before requesting personalized ads. Premium users do not receive ads in the app.

You can manage ad privacy choices from the app settings where available, or through your device and Google account ad settings.

6. Purchases and Premium Access

OmniNotes offers monthly, yearly, and lifetime Premium access through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google Play. RevenueCat verifies purchase status and communicates entitlement status to our backend. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

7. Permissions

The app may request permissions for features such as notifications, microphone recording, camera/gallery image selection, biometric unlock, and internet access. These permissions are used only for the related app features.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal notes. Data is shared only with the third-party providers listed above as needed to operate the app, process purchases, provide cloud sync, show ads to free users, comply with law, or protect the app and users.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local notes remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Premium cloud data remains in Supabase while your account exists and may be deleted upon request, subject to legal and operational requirements.

To request account or cloud data deletion, contact us at the email address below and include the Google account email used with OmniNotes.

10. Children’s Privacy

OmniNotes is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical measures such as local encryption, secure storage, HTTPS, authentication, access control, and Supabase row-level security to protect user data. No system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
