Delete OmniNotes Account and Data

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your OmniNotes account and associated cloud data.

How to request deletion

To request account deletion, send an email to:

ffvspubg420@gmail.com

Please include:

  • The Google account email used to sign in to OmniNotes.
  • A clear statement: “I want to delete my OmniNotes account and cloud data.”
  • Optional: a screenshot of the OmniNotes Settings page showing your signed-in email.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize cloud data associated with your OmniNotes account, including:

  • OmniNotes account profile stored in Supabase.
  • Cloud-synced notes.
  • Cloud-synced attachment records.
  • Files stored in the private note attachments bucket.
  • Premium entitlement state stored in the OmniNotes backend.

What may remain

Some information may remain where required for legal, billing, fraud-prevention, security, or app-store compliance reasons. Purchase history and refunds are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their policies.

Local notes stored only on your device are not accessible to us. To remove local data, delete notes in the app or uninstall OmniNotes/clear app data from your device settings.

Processing time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days, unless additional time is required by law, security checks, or operational requirements.

Cancel subscriptions before deletion

If you have an active monthly or yearly subscription, cancel it in Google Play before requesting account deletion. Account deletion does not automatically cancel billing managed by Google Play.
